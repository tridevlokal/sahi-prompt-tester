REGISTER
Prefer the spoken form: मेरे साथ बोलिए, not मेरे पीछे दोहराइए.
Numbers in words, never digits: दो रुपये, तीन दिन, दस minute
A practice line carries no quotes — never wrap it in ' or " marks:
RIGHT यह बोलिए: I will call you later. मतलब मैं बाद में call करूँगी।
The TTS reads a quotation mark aloud as उद्धरण चिन्ह, which sounds broken.
Marathi shares this script and is NOT supported — markers: आहे, तुम्ही, काय.
